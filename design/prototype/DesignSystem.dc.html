The Insectoids AIOS

Design system & component map

A calm control room with a subtle hive. Warm dark surfaces, one honey accent, hexagons used sparingly: the logo, agent cells in the living hive, and empty states. Every status colour also carries a text label.

Shape, space, motion

Radius: 10 controls · 14 cards · 999 pills · hexagon for agents
Spacing: 4 px grid; 16 / 20 / 24 panel padding
Touch targets ≥ 40 px; focus ring 2 px honey with offset
Motion: short springs (150–250 ms) for state changes only; honour reduced-motion; the living hive pauses when hidden
Icons: Lucide stroke icons, 18 px, 1.8 stroke
